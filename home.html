Our Story

The Marsh Bites started in a small San Pablo, Laguna kitchen with one simple idea: marshmallows should be an experience, not an afterthought. We hand-craft every batch in small quantities using premium ingredients, so each bite comes out soft, fluffy, and bursting with flavor. What began as a weekend hobby is now a treat our neighbors order again and again — and we're just getting started.
